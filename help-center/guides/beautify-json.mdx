## Overview

Beautifying JSON makes it easier to read and edit.

## How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open JSON Viewer AI">
    Visit [jsonviewer.ai](https://jsonviewer.ai)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your JSON">
    Copy your raw/minified JSON and paste it into the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Beautify">
    Press the "Beautify" button to reformat your JSON with proper indentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & Edit">
    Your JSON becomes more readable; make edits as needed before downloading or copying.
  </Step>
</Steps>
